Who Are We?

The idea behind ZetraTech.

👤 About Me

My name is Ziyad Alwesemy , and I am the founder and owner of ZetraTech. I created ZetraTech to make technology, device information, and digital security more accessible to everyone. My goal is to provide users with useful tools that help them understand their devices, identify potential security concerns, and learn more about their digital environment — completely free of charge. ZetraTech is built around my passion for technology, cybersecurity, and innovation, with the vision of creating a platform that makes digital safety and knowledge accessible to everyone.

🚀 What Is ZetraTech?

ZetraTech is a technology platform focused on device intelligence, digital protection, and modern computing. The platform brings device information, security checks, network intelligence, artificial intelligence, and specialized technology projects together in one place. The long-term vision is to grow ZetraTech into a complete digital technology ecosystem.
